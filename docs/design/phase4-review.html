How it works, in four layers:

  1. Sign-out is a plain form POST to /auth/logout. It is same-origin only, ends the session and returns a 303 to sign-in with Clear-Site-Data: "cache".
  2. After a successful sign-in, the browser loads /auth/continue as a new document. It clears the cache the same way and sends each role to its home.
  3. A small guard reloads a signed-in page if the browser ever restores it from the back/forward cache.
  4. Unchanged: passwords, rate limiting, sessions, the proxy and every server-side permission check.

What layers 1 and 3 cover: a full page reload alone would not have been enough. Protected pages are sent as public, max-age=0, which browsers may reuse from cache for Back. Clearing the cache on sign-out and sign-in covers that case, and the guard covers the back/forward cache.
