Design principles

The creative is the stage

The ad itself is the largest thing on its screen. Findings, evidence and history sit beside it, never above it.

One next action

Every screen makes the single next step for the viewer's role obvious, worded in plain language: "Respond to 2 findings", "Start review".

Four tones, one meaning each

Neutral, with Clyntique, needs the client, done. Severity uses a separate visual form, so colour never carries two meanings.

Record, not decoration

Version and round markers (V3 · R2) are set in mono as a recurring signature, the way a review ledger reads. Every decision shows who, when and on which version.

Colour

Your proposed palette, refined into roles. I added a muted red, used only for High severity and errors. Amber text on white fails contrast, so amber is a fill and marker colour, with a dark amber for text. Accent text passes WCAG AA on both white (4.9:1) and the canvas (4.6:1).

Components that change

Foundation (Stage C)

  • Tokens in globals.css replace all raw palette classes
  • Button, Field, Input, Select, Textarea, Checkbox chips
  • StatusPill (four tones) and SeverityMarker
  • PageHeader, Breadcrumb, SectionHeader
  • EmptyState, Skeleton, ErrorState, toast-free inline confirmations
  • AppShell: sidebar, mobile menu sheet, labelled sign-out

Review experience (Stage D)

  • Workspace layout: Stage + Panel with tabs
  • Version switcher and Compare mode on the stage
  • Finding item, response thread and decision ledger
  • Evidence list and upload form
  • Next-action block per role and state
  • Submission and remediation forms with a step indicator
